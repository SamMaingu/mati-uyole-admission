1. Lengo la Mfumo

Kutengeneza mfumo wa Online Admission utakaorahisisha mchakato wa kuomba admission kwa mwanafunzi na kurahisisha kazi za
Admission Office ya chuo.
Mfumo unaondoa utegemezi wa:

        Application forms za PDF
        Kuchapisha na kuscan forms
        Kutuma applications kwa email/post
        Manual checking ya taarifa
        Manual tracking ya application
        Mawasiliano yasiyo na status ya wazi
Mfumo una pande mbili kuu:
   1. Student/Applicant Portal
   2. Institute/Admission Management Portal
Architecture inaruhusu integrations za baadaye kama NECTA/NACTVET verification, payment APIs (GePG) na SMS services.

2. Principle Kuu ya Mfumo

   Simple for the student, powerful for the institute.

        Student asijazwe form ndefu na taarifa zisizohitajika wakati wa kuomba.
        Institute ipate tools za kutosha kusimamia applications, verification, selection na reporting.

3. Student/Applicant Process (Flow Iliyokamilika)

   Create Account  Personal Information  Academic Information
    Application Fee (Control Number)  Programme Selection
    Eligibility Check  Review  Submit  Track Status

Uamuzi wa mchakato (umethibitishwa): Application fee inalipwa kabla ya programme selection. Hii ni sawa na mifumo mingine ya
Tanzania (Mzumbe, OUT, UDSM) -- control number inatengenezwa mara baada ya registration/taarifa za msingi kukamilika, mwombaji
analipa, ndipo anaendelea kuchagua programme.

4. Step 1 -- Create Account

Required - First Name, Middle Name (kama ipo), Last Name - Phone Number - Password / Confirm Password
Optional - Email Address
Baada ya registration, system inatengeneza Application Number (mfano: MATI-2026-000124).
Phone number ni ya lazima kwa mawasiliano ya SMS. (Ongezeko: fikiria OTP/authentication code kwa first login badala ya password pekee
-- inasaidia maeneo yenye changamoto za password reset.)

5. Step 2 -- Personal Information

(Imesasishwa kulingana na Official Application Form 2026/2027 -- Section C)
Required: First/Middle/Last Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Place of Birth, Marital Status (Single/Married/Other), Citizenship
(Tanzanian/Others -- specify), Phone Number, Region, District, City/Town, Present Address, Postal Box
Optional: Email

Passport Photo: Upload wakati wa application (coloured, jina nyuma) -- hii ni tofauti na "certificates" nzito (birth cert, academic certs),
inahitajika mapema kwa ID na admission letter.

Disability Status (ongezeko kutoka form halisi): Yes/No; ikiwa Yes, eleza. Muhimu: hii ni kwa ajili ya institute kuandaa msaada
muafaka -- si sharti la kukubali/kukataa admission. Weka field hii kwa uangalifu (optional, na taarifa wazi kwa mwombaji kuhusu matumizi
yake).

Next of Kin (detailed zaidi kuliko draft ya awali): - Full Name, Relationship (mfano: Father), Phone Number - Present Address, Postal
Box, City/Town - Email Address

6. Step 3 -- Academic Information

(Imesasishwa kulingana na Official Application Form -- Section C)

        Qualification type (CSEE/Form IV, NVA Level III, Diploma/NTA, Other)
        Index Numbers mbili (form halisi inaomba zote):

                 Form IV/CSEE Index No. -- format S0000/0000/0000
                 Index No. ya pili -- format P... (huenda ni PSLE -- thibitisha na institute maana yake hasa)
        Division na Points za matokeo ya NECTA (mfano: Division II, points 24)
        Year Completed, Secondary School Name, Country (kwa waliosoma nje)
        Primary School: Jina, District, Region (hii ilikuwa haipo kwenye draft ya awali -- form halisi inaihitaji)

Ongezeko muhimu -- Index Number & AVN validation: - Weka field validation kwa format za index number hapo juu - Kwa waombaji
wenye Diploma/NTA wanaoendelea (NTA5NTA6), ongeza field ya AVN (Award Verification Number) kutoka NACTVET (mfano:
19NA12212ME), hata kama verification bado ni manual kwa sasa - Muhimu: Kwa mujibu wa form halisi, NACTVET verification rasmi
inafanyika BAADA ya selection, siyo wakati wa application. Kwa hiyo automatic eligibility check ya system (Sehemu ya 9) ni preliminary
tu based on taarifa alizoingiza mwombaji mwenyewe -- siyo verified na NACTVET mpaka baada ya kuchaguliwa.

Certificate upload si mandatory wakati wa initial application (certificates zinahitajika baadaye -- ona Sehemu 14).

7. Step 4 -- Application Fee

Fee: TZS 20,000 -- Non-refundable (kwa mwaka 2026/2027)

Application Fee  Generate Control Number  Student Anapiga Malipo
 Payment Verification  PAID

Payment Status (imepanuliwa): - Unpaid - Pending - Paid - Failed - Expired (control number za GePG/mobile money zina muda wa
kuisha -- ongezeko)

Business Rule: Student hawezi kuchagua programme mpaka fee iwe PAID.

Ongezeko -- Non-Tanzanian applicants: Weka fee tofauti/utaratibu tofauti kwa waombaji wa kigeni (kama UDSM: TZS kwa Tanzania,
USD kwa foreign), kwani doc ya awali haikuwa na tofauti hii.

Uamuzi unaohitajika -- Control Number (muhimu kabla ya FRD):

Mchakato wa sasa (manual, form halisi) unatumia control number MOJA ya pamoja kwa waombaji wote (997400027916 kupitia
NMB/GePG), na verification inafanyika kwa kuangalia risiti iliyowasilishwa (manual, siyo automatic per-applicant).

Kwa online system, chagua moja:

   1. Endelea na control number ya pamoja -- rahisi zaidi kutumia GePG setup iliyopo sasa, lakini payment verification inabaki manual
        (Finance Officer ana-cross-check risiti/reference number dhidi ya jina la mwombaji)

   2. Omba control number za kipekee kwa kila mwombaji kutoka NMB/GePG -- inaruhusu automatic verification, lakini inahitaji
        mpangilio mpya wa kibiashara na benki/GePG kabla ya development kuanza

Hii ni uamuzi wa kibiashara (business decision) na chuo, siyo teknolojia pekee -- thibitisha kabla ya kuandika FRD kwa sababu inaathiri
database design ya Payment module.

Architecture iwe tayari kwa GePG / payment API integration bila kujali chaguo litakalochukuliwa.

18­19. Application Management & Review

Admission Officer anaweza: search/filter (Application No, Name, Phone, Programme, Gender, Region, District, Payment status, Application
status), review taarifa zote sehemu moja, change status, request additional info, approve/reject.
Ongezeko -- Duplicate check: Zuia mwombaji mmoja (Index Number/phone moja) kuomba mara mbili kwa programme tofauti mwaka
mmoja bila sababu za msingi.

20. Selection Management

Admin anaweka academic year, programme, capacity, selection criteria. System inasaidia kupanga applicants; final approval inabaki
kwa authorized staff.

21. Admission Letter

Auto-generated: institute details, applicant name, Application No, programme, NTA level, academic year, reporting info. Baadaye: QR
Code verification.

22. SMS Notifications

SMS kwa: account creation, payment confirmed, submission, status update, selection, documents required. SMS ni primary channel; email
ni supplementary. Abstraction layer ili provider iweze kubadilishwa.

23. Institute Reports

Application Report (by programme, gender, region, district, eligibility, selection) na Payment Report -- exportable Excel/CSV/PDF.

27. Important Design Rules

   1. Student asiulizwe taarifa isiyohitajika wakati wa hatua hiyo
   2. Application iwe short and simple
   3. Programme selection ifunguke baada ya application fee payment confirmation (imethibitishwa -- sawa na mifumo mingine

        ya Tanzania)
   4. Documents nyingi ziwe admission/registration stage, siyo initial application
   5. Student aone progress na status muda wote
   6. SMS ni primary notification channel, email ni supplementary
   7. Admission requirements ziwe configurable
   8. Payment, SMS, academic verification, document verification ziwe modules zinazo-integrate APIs baadaye
   9. System iwe mobile-first
 10. Final admission decision ibaki kwa authorized institute officials

28. MVP -- Tunachojenga Kwanza

Student: Registration, Login, Dashboard, Personal/Academic Info, Payment, Programme Selection, Eligibility Check, Review, Submit,
Status Tracking

Institute: Admin login, Dashboard, Programme Management, Requirements, Application Management/Review, Payment Monitoring,
Eligibility, Selection, Status Management, Reports, SMS

Later: Document upload baada ya selection, Admission letters, QR verification, NECTA/NACTVET integration, GePG integration, Advanced
SMS, Waitlist automation, Appeal/re-application flow

29. Final System Flow

Student: Create Account  Personal Info  Academic Info  Pay Fee  Programme Selection  Eligibility Check  Review  Submit 
Track Status  Selection/Waitlist  Upload Documents  Admission/Registration

Institute: Configure Academic Year  Set Application Windows  Add Programmes  Set Requirements  Receive Applications 
Payment Monitoring  Review  Eligibility Verification  Selection  Approval  Notify Students  Admission Documents  Registration

30. Core Vision

   A simple digital admission workflow connecting the applicant and the institute -- from application to admission.

Applicant: hatua chache, lugha rahisi, mobile-friendly, anaona status yake kila wakati.
Institute: centralized applications, verification, payment tracking, selection, communication na reports sehemu moja.
